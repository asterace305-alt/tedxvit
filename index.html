WHAT IS TED?

A Global Movement for Ideas That Shift Civilizations

Founded in 1984 as a conference bringing together Technology, Entertainment, and Design, TED has evolved into a worldwide platform covering science, business, arts, and global issues in over 100 languages.

6,000+Worldwide events hosted
18 MinsGold standard talk limit
3 Billion+Global talk video views
WHAT IS TEDx?

Self-Organized Catalysts Empowering Local Voices

In the spirit of ideas worth spreading, TEDx is a program of local, self-organized events that bring communities together to share a TED-like experience under an official license from TED.

170+Participating countries
100%Non-profit volunteer driven
GrassrootsSpotlighting regional genius
WHAT IS TEDxVITPune?

A Decade of Milestone Ideas at VIT Pune

Established with a vision to ignite intellectual curiosity in Maharashtra’s cultural and educational capital, TEDxVITPune has grown into Pune’s premier student-led intellectual conference, celebrating its 10th milestone edition.
